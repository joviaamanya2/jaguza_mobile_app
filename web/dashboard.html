A farmer reported their cattle showing high fever (40.5C), swollen lymph nodes, and nasal discharge. What could this be?
Based on the symptoms – high fever, swollen lymph nodes, and nasal discharge – this most likely indicates East Coast Fever (ECF) caused by Theileria parva, transmitted by brown ear ticks.

Recommended Actions:
1. Administer Buparvaquone (Butalex) – 2.5mg/kg IM
2. Isolate the affected animal
3. Apply tick grease to all animals
4. Contact Dr. Amina Ochieng for on-site evaluation

ECF is fatal if untreated within 3 to 5 days. Act immediately!
